The same seeded quarter, drawn two ways. Planning should make the ladder — Career Goal, then Quarterly Objective, then Outcome — impossible to misread, with rank, gaps and Effort as the tools. Implementation should feel like a board you move work across, with counts and Effort as the tools. If the two views read as the same screen with the columns rearranged, this design has failed.
